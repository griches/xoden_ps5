Connect your games to XoDen.

Sign in with Microsoft. Your PS5 saves the result automatically.

Google Chrome must be installed. Microsoft sign-in opens in a temporary Chrome window. You can keep Safari or another browser as your default.

  1. On your PS5, open Settings → Xbox account → Sign in with Microsoft.
  2. Enter the address and pairing code shown on the TV.
  3. Sign in to Microsoft in the window that opens.

16 letters and numbers, shown on the TV. The code changes each time.
